Box Score

Yardage is derived automatically from each play's down, distance, and yard-line entries — no separate yards field to fill in. Plays with a penalty, a turnover, or a sack aren't counted toward rushing/passing yards (those get their own rows instead — penalty yards, turnovers, and sack yards). First downs are detected when the down resets to 1st within the same drive.
